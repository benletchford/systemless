The complete index of titles offered for download inside this Mac app release. Bring-your-own archives remain the main experience.

    Age guidance 4+: Abstract brick-breaking puzzle.

  • GNU Chess Mac 4.0b5

    Play chess against the Macintosh port of GNU Chess, with an opening book, adjustable clocks and a move list.

    Age guidance 4+: Traditional chess board game; no wagering.

    Play Go on a full-size board against the GNU Go 2.6 engine.

    Age guidance 4+: Traditional Go board game; no wagering.
