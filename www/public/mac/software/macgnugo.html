MacGnuGo

Play Go on a full-size board against the GNU Go 2.6 engine.

Age guidance 4+

Traditional Go board game; no wagering. This is local content guidance, not an Apple rating. No age declaration is requested for this selection.

Edition and limitations

Original GPLv2 MacGnuGo 0.5 GUI and GNU Go 2.6 package, 68K. This historical version has original gameplay limitations; Mac sources are included and matching engine source is available below.
