Damage Incorporated

Lead a fire team through close-quarters missions where every order competes with the next burst of gunfire.

Paranoid Productions · 1997 · Four-level demo

Age guidance 18+

First-person shooter with violence; mature content.

This is conservative guidance for access in Systemless, rather than a regional classification certificate. Restricted titles require a suitable locally declared age range in the app’s Settings. No date of birth is requested.

Open in Systemless

Open this title’s information in Systemless for Mac. On a Mac with Systemless installed, a supported universal link opens the title’s details. It never downloads or runs the game automatically. This page is the browser fallback.

If the link remains in your browser, open Systemless, dismiss the free launch reminder if shown, and use Cloud → Starter Games to find the title. Enable the optional catalogue in Settings if needed.

Edition and limits

Three single-player levels and one network level. Distributed unchanged with the original Read This notice.

Starter downloads are optional and count toward the free library’s one-title limit. The Mac unlock does not buy a full game or remove its original trial or registration terms. Compatibility and availability may change.
