A few things to know.

Do I need a ROM or a classic Mac OS installation?

No. Systemless independently reimplements the classic Macintosh environment. You do not need an Apple ROM image or a classic Mac OS installation.

Will all my old software work?

Compatibility varies by application and version. Some titles need particular display or control settings; others are not supported yet. See Mac support for help.

Does the app include the website’s entire game library?

No. The Mac app has a separate starter selection. You can import other software you are entitled to use; its original licence and registration terms still apply.

Can I try Systemless without installing it?

Yes. Browse the website to play supported titles in your browser. The Mac app keeps its own local library and saves.
