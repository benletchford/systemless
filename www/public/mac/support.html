Systemless for Mac

Bring your classics home.

Help with your free, unlimited library and classic software.

Import and open your software

Drop an archive into Systemless or use Import Game… in the menu. Supported containers include .sit, .hqx, .bin, ZIP archives and supported disk images. Finder’s Open With → Systemless can import and launch a supported archive.

Systemless runs compatible classic 68K and PowerPC Macintosh software. Compatibility varies by title and version. If a program needs a particular display depth, architecture or key mapping, choose its Game Settings… in your library.

Free, unlimited imports

The initial Mac release is free, with unlimited imports and no in-app purchases, subscription or launch reminders. Reimporting the same archive does not create a duplicate. No Systemless account is required.

Your files and progress

Your imported archives, library settings and saved progress are stored on your Mac. Installed software works without the optional online catalogue. Keep a backup of data you want to retain before removing local application data.

Starter games and content controls

The starter-software index lists every title offered by the Mac app, with edition, archive identity, original terms and age guidance. The initial release offers Diamonds 2.0 Demo, GNU Chess Mac and MacGnuGo: an abstract puzzle and two traditional board games. No age declaration is requested for this starter selection. Wumpus and Damage Incorporated are not offered by this Mac release.

Use Report a content or rights concern in a title’s details or Settings to prepare an email. You choose what to send. There is no user chat or user-posted content in the Mac catalogue.

Original software and licences

Systemless independently reimplements the classic Macintosh environment. It does not include or require an Apple ROM image or a classic Mac OS installation.

Import software you are entitled to use and follow its original licence and registration terms. Systemless does not grant rights to third-party games, applications, ROMs or operating-system files.

The public runtime core is open source. The Mac app includes component notices under Licences and Credits… in its menu and Settings.

Get help or report a concern

Email ben@letchford.cloud with your Systemless version, macOS version, software title and version, and what happened. A screenshot or the exact error message helps. Only send files you have permission to share.

Use the same address to report a catalogue, privacy or rights concern. Include the title and relevant link so the issue can be investigated.
