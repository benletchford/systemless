## Into Sosaria

![The Ultima III shareware party on Sosaria's overworld](https://assets.systemless.org/catalogue/media/sha256/45/45aa130edcd79906a0f5a4b32284001937a016e7f9460d762bbbf1bb4e8ef1a5.png)

Gather a party, explore towns and dungeons, and survive the road across
Sosaria in LairWare's colorful Macintosh adaptation of *Ultima III*. The
original v1.3 archive includes a sample party, so you can set out from the
title screen without creating characters first.

This is the unregistered shareware release. Its included Read Me First allows
the complete, unchanged archive to be shared, and explains that unregistered
players cannot travel to Ambrosia or raise their abilities. No registration
code is included or applied here.

The unchanged archive reaches the overworld in both Systemless and BasiliskII.
Browser launch remains disabled until manual review of the promoted archive.
